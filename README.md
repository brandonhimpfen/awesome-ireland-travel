## Official & Planning

Essential official tourism portals, entry requirements, visa guidelines, and heritage management sites.

- [Ireland.com (Fáilte Ireland)](https://www.ireland.com/) - Official national tourism portal featuring regional itineraries, scenic drive guides, and cultural highlights.
- [Department of Justice - Visas & Immigration](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/) - Official government portal for visa entry requirements, passport guidelines, and immigration rules for visitors.
- [Office of Public Works (Heritage Ireland)](https://heritageireland.ie/) - Official management authority for national monuments, historic properties, castles, and archaeological sites across Ireland.
- [Met Éireann Weather](https://www.met.ie/) - Official national meteorological service providing forecasts, marine warnings, and regional climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating national railway networks, intercity bus routes, public transit cards, and air travel.

- [Iarnród Éireann (Irish Rail)](https://www.irishrail.ie/) - National passenger train operator connecting Dublin with major cities including Cork, Galway, Limerick, and Waterford.
- [Bus Éireann](https://www.buseireann.ie/) - National bus and coach network operating regional and intercity routes across Ireland.
- [TFI Transport for Ireland](https://www.transportforireland.ie/) - Integrated public transport authority managing Leap Card ticketing, urban buses, commuter rails, and Luas trams in Dublin.
- [Aer Lingus](https://www.aerlingus.com/) - National flag carrier operating international long-haul flights and European connections through Dublin Airport (DUB).

## Destinations & Regions

### Dublin & East Coast

- [Visit Dublin (Official City Guide)](https://www.visitdublin.com/) - Official municipal portal for exploring Trinity College, Temple Bar, museums, and historic distillery trails.
- [Dublin Bus / TFI Real Time](https://www.transportforireland.ie/) - Official real-time transit planner for navigating Dublin's urban bus network.

### Wild Atlantic Way & West Coast

- [Wild Atlantic Way Official Guide](https://www.ireland.com/en-us/things-to-do/wild-atlantic-way/) - Comprehensive route planner for the 2,500 km coastal touring route spanning from Donegal to West Cork.
- [Cliffs of Moher Visitor Experience](https://www.cliffsofmoher.ie/) - Official visitor portal for exploring Co. Clare's dramatic sea cliffs, coastal walking paths, and visitor center.
- [Discover Galway](https://galwaytourism.ie/) - Regional visitor guide for Galway City, Connemara National Park, and the Aran Islands ferry points.

### South & Ancient East

- [Pure Cork Tourism](https://www.purecork.ie/) - Official regional guide for Cork City, Blarney Castle, Ring of Kerry links, and West Cork coastal villages.
- [Ireland's Ancient East Portal](https://www.ireland.com/en-us/destinations/irelands-ancient-east/) - Visitor hub exploring 5,000 years of history through Newgrange, Kilkenny Castle, and monastic sites.

### Northern Ireland

- [Discover Northern Ireland](https://www.discovernorthernireland.com/) - Official tourism guide for Belfast, the Giant's Causeway, Derry-Londonderry, and Titanic Belfast.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers, remote professionals, and digital nomads in Ireland.

- [Remote working in Ireland (Enterprise Ireland)](https://enterprise.gov.ie/) - Official government portal detailing remote working hubs, co-working spaces, and digital infrastructure across rural and urban towns.
- [Dogpatch Labs Dublin](https://dogpatchlabs.com/) - Leading startup hub and coworking space located in the historic CHQ Building in Dublin.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, driving on the left, and rural safety.

- [999 / 112 Emergency Services](https://www.gov.ie/) - Direct nationwide emergency hotline for police (An Garda Síochána), fire, and ambulance assistance.
- [Mountain Rescue Ireland](https://www.mountainrescue.ie/) - Volunteer mountain rescue network offering safety advice for hikers exploring upland trails and national parks.
